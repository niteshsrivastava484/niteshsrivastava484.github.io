About

Predictable delivery, at scale

I am a Director, Delivery Management and Release Train Engineer at FICO. I lead delivery governance for 13 concurrent product workstreams on FICO’s Next Gen Platform, a cloud-native decisioning platform. That work covers 100+ engineers and 7+ Scrum Masters and RTEs across India and the US. We delivered the M0 and M1 milestones on schedule, and the platform is on track for General Availability in October 2026.

I started out as a software engineer in 2004 and spent my early years in quality engineering. That included more than five years as QA Manager on FICO's banking and financial-services decisioning products. At Infosys I moved into program and client leadership. I ran a $1.6M+/year revenue portfolio and a 50+ engineer organization, delivering enterprise transformation and platform modernization programs for Deutsche Bank and Unilever. That work included automating 5,000+ SAP interfaces and setting up Testing Centers of Excellence on both accounts.

Two decades of this have taught me that large programs rarely fail on technology. They fail on dependencies, alignment and clarity. So I focus on making large-scale delivery predictable through strong dependency management, execution discipline and governance. I also coach Scrum Masters, RTEs and delivery leads, because a strong delivery system needs strong people running it.

Coaching & Leadership

Growing the people who grow teams

Besides running delivery, I coach the Scrum Masters and agile coaches who lead the teams day to day.

01

Coaching the coaches

One-to-one and group coaching for Scrum Masters and RTEs. We work on facilitation, metrics literacy, handling conflict and influencing without authority.

02

A common operating model

Shared delivery standards across teams: Definition of Ready, sprint-health signals, escalation paths and reporting rhythms, so every team is measured the same way.

03

Data-informed retrospectives

Retros that start from evidence: velocity, spillover, blockers and quality signals. Teams then commit to a few focused actions instead of a long wish-list.

04

Leading distributed teams

Building trust and alignment across time zones with clear ownership, open dependency boards, and steady, low-ceremony communication.

AI-Augmented Delivery

Using AI agents to strengthen delivery

I build and use LLM-powered agents connected to Jira and Confluence. They take over routine delivery analysis, so Scrum Masters and leaders can spend their time on decisions.

Sprint Pulse & Health

A daily automated brief for each team: sprint RAG status, stalled work, and wins, plus suggested stand-up questions for the Scrum Master.

Blocker & Dependency Radar

Continuously maps "blocks / is blocked by" relationships across teams, flags cross-team dependencies that are late or unscheduled, and publishes an up-to-date blocker view.

Backlog Readiness Scoring

Scores every backlog item against quality checks such as description, acceptance criteria, estimates, ownership and linkage. Incomplete work gets caught before planning, not during the sprint.

Spillover Risk Prediction

Spots sprint commitments likely to spill over, while there's still time to re-plan or escalate.

PI Planning Readiness

Checks how complete the epics are, team capacity, dependency readiness and risk posture before a Program Increment, so planning days stay focused.

Retros & Executive Reporting

Drafts retrospective summaries, risk-escalation memos and leadership slide decks from live delivery data. This cuts hours of manual reporting every sprint.

Built with LLM agents · Model Context Protocol (MCP) · Jira & Confluence REST APIs · Python · python-pptx / openpyxl
